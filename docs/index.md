<h2 id="about-heading">About this talk</h2>

As AI systems become deeply embedded in software development, the question
isn't whether machines will assist us — it's how we ensure they do so
responsibly. Trustworthy AI isn't just about algorithms; it's about preserving
human judgment, creativity, and ethics in every loop.

This site is a companion to a 45-minute talk. Each chapter pairs the slide you
saw on screen with the talk track you heard — so you can revisit it, share it,
or read it cold without ever having been in the room.
