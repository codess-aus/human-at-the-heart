# Chapter 10 — Part Two: Principles

We've covered the loop. Now we turn to the criteria.

Loops keep humans in the right place. Principles tell us what we're trying to
*be true* about the system the human is participating in. They're the
short-list of properties we won't compromise on — the things that, if they
slip, the whole "trustworthy" claim falls down.

The next six chapters lay out the three principles we treat as
non-negotiable, then connect them to Microsoft's broader Responsible AI
Standard and finally to how GitHub operationalises all of it inside Copilot.

Think of this section as the answer to the question: *what does "ready to
ship" mean when the thing you're shipping is a model?*
