# Chapter 04 — What We'll Cover

Three threads, in order.

**Human-in-the-Loop design.** Where does the human belong when an AI is
proposing the work? We'll look at the loop itself — what review, approval, and
override actually mean in a product like Copilot, and why those checkpoints
aren't optional.

**Principles of trustworthy AI.** The three non-negotiables — explainability,
reliability, inclusivity — and how they map onto Microsoft's Responsible AI
Standard. These are the criteria we use to decide whether a feature is *ready
to ship* rather than just *technically working*.

**Real-world practices.** What governance, empathy, and measurement look like
when a team is shipping AI on Monday morning. Practical questions you can take
back to your own product — including a four-question checklist for the next
feature you put in front of a customer.

If you remember nothing else from the slides, the practical checklist is the
one to take home.
