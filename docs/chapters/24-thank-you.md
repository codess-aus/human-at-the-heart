# Chapter 24 — Thank You

Thank you for spending 45 minutes with this material.

If you take one thing from the talk, take the four-question checklist from
chapter 20 — and ask it of the next AI feature your team is about to put in
front of a customer.

Questions, challenges, and counter-arguments are very welcome — in the room
if we have time, and afterwards in the repo or wherever you find me. If
something here landed, take it back to your team. If something didn't, push
back; the conversation is how this work gets better.

Either way, keep the humans at the heart of it.
