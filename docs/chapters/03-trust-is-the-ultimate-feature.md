# Chapter 03 — Trust Is the Ultimate Feature

One sentence that frames everything else.

We ship a lot of features. We benchmark them, we A/B test them, we celebrate
their launches. But the feature that actually decides whether an AI product
survives contact with real users is not on any of those dashboards.

It's **trust**.

If a developer doesn't trust a suggestion, they won't accept it — and the
fastest model in the world is worthless if its output gets thrown away. If a
team doesn't trust a workflow, they'll build a parallel one without it. If a
regulator doesn't trust the system, the system doesn't ship at all.

Trust isn't a marketing layer added at the end. It's a design constraint that
starts on day one, and it's what we're going to dismantle into something
practical over the next twenty-one slides.
