# Chapter 15 — Microsoft's Responsible AI Standard

The three non-negotiables don't exist in a vacuum. Inside Microsoft they sit
on top of the **Responsible AI Standard** — a published, enforced framework
that every product team, including the ones shipping Copilot, has to meet
before release.

It spells out six commitments:

- **Fairness** — outcomes are equitable across the people the system affects.
- **Reliability & Safety** — the system behaves as intended, including under
  conditions its designers didn't anticipate.
- **Privacy & Security** — data is handled with the rigour the user assumed
  when they gave it to us.
- **Inclusiveness** — the system is designed for the full range of people
  who'll use it, not the median user.
- **Transparency** — people can understand what the system is doing and why.
- **Accountability** — there is always a human or team answerable for the
  system's behaviour.

You'll notice they map cleanly onto explainability, reliability, and
inclusivity — but with the additional weight of being **operationalised**.
This isn't an ethics statement; it's a gate. Features that don't meet it
don't ship.
