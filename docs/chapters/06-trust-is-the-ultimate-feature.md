# Chapter 06 — Trust Is the Ultimate Feature

So we come back to the line we started with.

**Trust is the ultimate feature.** Build it in. Ship it on purpose.

That phrasing matters. *On purpose*. Trustworthy AI doesn't emerge as a
side-effect of using the right model or the right framework. It is the result
of a long series of deliberate decisions — about where the human sits in the
loop, about which principles you refuse to compromise on, about which
practices you make the path of least resistance for your team.

If you do that work — if you put the human at the heart, hold the line on
explainability, reliability and inclusivity, and operationalise governance
without friction — you will ship AI features that people actually rely on.
Not features that wow them in a demo and disappoint them in production.
Features they trust.

That is the difference between an AI product that lasts and one that doesn't.
