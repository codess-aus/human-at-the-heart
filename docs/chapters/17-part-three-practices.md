# Chapter 17 — Part Three: Practices

We've covered the loop. We've covered the principles. The last section is
about *practice* — how a team operationalises all of this when the sprint is
short, the backlog is long, and the model is changing under their feet.

The next six chapters are deliberately practical. Governance you can actually
live with. Empathy as a workflow constraint. A four-question checklist for
the next AI feature you put in front of customers. A view on what to measure.
And a closing reminder that, inside any AI-assisted team, *the developer is
the loop*.

If you've come for the takeaways, this is the part to take home.
