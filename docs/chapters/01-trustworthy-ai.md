# Chapter 01 — Trustworthy AI

> **A GitHub + Microsoft perspective on building AI that preserves human
> judgment, creativity, and ethics in every loop.**

Welcome. This is a talk about trust — specifically, the trust we extend to the
intelligent systems that increasingly sit between us and our work.

The last few years have changed software development more profoundly than any
period since the move to the cloud. Generative AI now writes code alongside us,
reviews pull requests, drafts documentation, and — at its most ambitious —
takes autonomous actions on our behalf.

That shift raises the question every engineering organisation now has to
answer: when the assistant in our editor is shaping decisions that affect
production, our customers, and the people who use what we build — **how do we
make sure those decisions are trustworthy?**

Over the next 45 minutes we'll work through one answer to that question,
drawn from how GitHub and Microsoft build and ship AI products at scale.
