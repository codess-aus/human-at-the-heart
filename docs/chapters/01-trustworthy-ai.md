# Chapter 01 — Trustworthy AI

> **A GitHub + Microsoft perspective on building AI that preserves human
> judgment, creativity, and ethics in every loop.**

Welcome. This is a talk about trust — specifically, the trust we extend to the
intelligent systems that increasingly sit between us and our work.

The last few years have changed software development more profoundly than any
period since the move to the cloud. Generative AI is no longer a curiosity in a
research paper. It writes code alongside us, reviews our pull requests, drafts
our documentation, and — at its most ambitious — takes autonomous actions on
our behalf.

That shift raises a question every engineering organisation now has to answer:

> When the assistant in our editor is making decisions that affect production,
> our customers, and the people who use what we build — **how do we make sure
> those decisions are trustworthy?**

Over the next forty-five minutes we'll work through that question together, with
a particular focus on the principles GitHub and Microsoft apply to the products
millions of developers use every day. The aim isn't to convince you that AI is
safe; it's to give you a practical toolkit for *making* it safe in the systems
you ship.

We'll move through three parts:

- **Human-in-the-Loop design** — why human oversight is not a nice-to-have
- **The principles of trustworthy AI** — explainability, reliability, inclusivity
- **The practices** — how to embed governance and empathy without slowing the team down

By the end you will have a checklist you can use on Monday morning, on whatever
AI feature you're about to ship.
