# Chapter 05 — Part One: Human-in-the-Loop

The first of three parts.

"Human-in-the-Loop" is a phrase you'll hear a lot in AI conversations, and
it's at risk of becoming a slogan. Over the next four chapters we'll try to
make it concrete — not as a posture, but as a specific design pattern with
specific consequences for how a feature behaves.

The thesis is straightforward. AI is extraordinary at *generation* — drafting,
suggesting, exploring, summarising. It is much less good at *judgment* —
deciding what should go into production, what's appropriate for this customer,
what trade-offs are acceptable for this team.

So we keep the generative power on tap, and we keep the judgment with people.
Everything that follows is an answer to the question: *what does that look
like in the product?*
